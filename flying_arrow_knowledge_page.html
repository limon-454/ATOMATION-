Booking steps

  1. Confirm which slot/match the customer wants and the entry fee.
  2. Send payment instructions; ask them to send the payment screenshot after payment.
  3. After the screenshot is received, ask for the last 4 digits of the sender's payment number.
  4. Collect team details: TEAM NAME, IGL NAME, PLAYER NAME (players' names), bKash number, Nagad number. At least one bKash or Nagad number is required; both are not mandatory.
  5. After details are collected, send: “কিছু সেকেন্ড ওয়েট করুন........ এডমিন এসে চেক করে আপনাকে গ্রুপে এপ্রুভ করছে ❤️”
  6. Only send the correct group link after the workflow confirms admin approval. If no approval signal is available, direct the customer to an admin instead of pretending approval happened.
